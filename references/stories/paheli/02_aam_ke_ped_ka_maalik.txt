Aam ke ped ka maalik (lok katha)

Do padosi ek aam ke ped par daava karte hain. Dono kehte hain ped unhone lagaya. Koi gawah nahi. Chatur kehta hai: "Theek hai,
ped kaat kar lakdi aadhi-aadhi baant do." Pehla padosi khush ho jaata hai, "theek hai". Doosra ro padta hai: "Mat kaato,
ped use de do, par ise zinda rehne do. Maine ise bachpan se paani diya hai."
Chatur faisla deta hai: jo ped ko bachana chahta hai, wahi asli maalik hai.
Part 1: jhagda, suraag (ek ped ko pyaar se dekhta hai, doosra sirf phalon ki baat karta hai), Chatur ka "kaat do" wala aadesh.
Part 2: dono ki pratikriya, faisla. Seekh: jo sach me apna hota hai, uska nuksaan koi nahi dekh sakta.
