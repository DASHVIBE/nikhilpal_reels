Chor ki daadhi mein tinka (lok katha)

Raja ki anguthi darbar se gayab ho gayi. Us samay darbar me sirf darbari the. Chatur ne kaha: "Maharaj, chor yahin hai,
aur mujhe pata chal gaya hai. Chor ki daadhi me ek tinka atka hai!" Turant ek darbari ne ghabrakar apni daadhi par haath
phera. Chatur ne usi ki taraf ishara kiya. Talaashi me anguthi usi ke paas mili.
Part 1: anguthi gayab, saare darbari kasam khate hain, Chatur kehta hai "chor mil gaya", ruk jao.
Part 2: tinke wali baat, daadhi par haath, anguthi baramad. Seekh: chor ka mann hi use pakadwa deta hai.
