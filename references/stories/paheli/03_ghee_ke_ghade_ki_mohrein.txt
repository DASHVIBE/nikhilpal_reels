Ghee ke ghade ki mohrein (lok katha)

Ek vyapari tirth yatra par jaane se pehle ek ghada padosi ke paas rakh gaya. Upar ghee tha, neeche sone ki mohrein chhupi
thi. Paanch saal baad lauta to ghade me sirf ghee nikla. Padosi bola: "Ye to ghee ka hi ghada tha." Darbar me case aaya.
Chatur ne ghee pighla kar dekha: tale me ek purani mohar chipki mili. Phir Chatur ne poocha: "Is ghade ka ghee paanch
saal purana hai?" Ghee taaza tha, yaani padosi ne ghada khaali karke naya ghee bhara tha. Padosi pakda gaya.
Part 1: vyapari ka daava, padosi ki kasam, suraag (ghade ka ghee taaza khushboo deta hai).
Part 2: ghee pighlana, tale ki mohar, taaze ghee ka tark, faisla. Seekh: amaanat me khayanat ka sach chhupta nahi.
