Vidur ka saag (Mahabharat, Udyog Parv ki prasiddh katha)

Shanti doot bankar Shri Krishna Hastinapur aaye. Duryodhan ne unke liye chhappan bhog aur mahal sajaya, par Krishna ne
uska nimantran thukra diya: "Jahan prem na ho, wahan ka bhojan nahi karte." Wo seedhe Mahatma Vidur ki saadi kutiya
pahunche. Vidur ghar par nahi the; Vidurani prem me itni magn ho gayi ki kele chheel kar chhilke Krishna ko dene lagi aur
kele phenkti gayi. Krishna muskura kar chhilke khaate rahe. Vidur lautke ghabra gaye, par Krishna bole: "Aisa swaad
Duryodhan ke chhappan bhog me bhi nahi." Phir Vidur ne saada saag-roti khilaya.
Seekh: Bhagwan bhaav ke bhookhe hain, dhan aur dikhawe ke nahi.
(Engine me Vidurani nahi ban sakti: kahani Krishna Arjun ko sunate hain; P = Vidur.)
