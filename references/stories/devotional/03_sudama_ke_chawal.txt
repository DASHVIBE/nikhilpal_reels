Sudama ke chawal (Bhagavat Puran, Dasham Skandh)

Sandipani ashram ke dost Sudama bahut garib the. Patni ke kehne par wo Dwarka gaye, bhent me sirf ek potli chiwda (chawal)
le gaye, jo dene me sharma rahe the. Krishna ne door se dekhte hi daud kar gale lagaya, apne haathon se pair dhoye.
Phir potli khud cheen kar ek mutthi chiwda khaya: Sudama ko ek lok mil gaya; doosri mutthi par doosra. Teesri mutthi par
Rukmini ne haath pakad liya. Sudama ne kuch maanga nahi, khaali haath laute... par ghar pahunche to jhopdi mahal ban chuki thi.
Seekh: Sachchi dosti me lena-dena nahi hota; Bhagwan bina maange bhakt ki zaroorat jaante hain.
(Kahani Krishna Arjun ko sunate hain, ya Sudama Krishna se milte hain; P = Sudama.)
