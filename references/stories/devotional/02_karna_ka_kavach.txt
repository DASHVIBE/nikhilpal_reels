Karna ka kavach-kundal daan (Mahabharat, Van Parv)

Karna janm se suraj dev ka diya kavach aur kundal pehne the; jab tak wo shareer par the, Karna ko koi maar nahi sakta tha.
Karna ka niyam tha: suraj ki pooja ke baad jo bhi maange, use khaali haath nahi lautata. Suraj dev ne sapne me chetaavni di:
"Indra brahmin banke kavach maangne aayenge, mat dena." Agle din ek brahmin aaya aur kavach-kundal maange. Karna jaan gaye
ki ye Indra hain, phir bhi apne shareer se kaat kar kavach-kundal de diye. Indra sharminda hue aur badle me ek amogh
shakti di.
Seekh: Daan wahi jo apne nuksaan ki parwah kiye bina diya jaye. Isliye Karna "daanveer" kehlaye.
(Krishna Arjun ko ye raaz sunate hain ki Karna kitne mahaan the; P = Karna.)
